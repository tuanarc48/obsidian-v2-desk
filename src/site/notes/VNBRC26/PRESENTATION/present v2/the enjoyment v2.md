DISCOVER

My first step was not choosing my favorite coffee, it was imagine what make a cup truly enjoyable : a floral aroma, layered tropical fruit acidity, candy-like sweetness, and a round - juicy mouthfeel consistent at all temperature.

So for the coffee I bring here is a blend of two Panama Geshas:

Four gram of Honey process Gesha from [[VNBRC26/BEAN SELECTION/Alto Bambito\|Alto Bambito]], give the blend gentle floral, and lively acidity of stone fruits

Nine gram of an Washed process Gesha from Finca Los [[VNBRC26/BEAN SELECTION/Cenizos W\|Cenizos W]] , gives the blend the berry like acidity, candy - like sweetness, adds the round - smooth mouthfeel to my cup.

This blend is built on 70% Cenizos and 30% Alto Bambito. I kept Cenizos as the primary coffee to preserve its delicate floral notes and deep sweetness, while adding just enough Alto Bambito to lift the cup with a bright, complex layer of acidity





</div></div>


<div class="transclusion internal-embed is-loaded"><div class="markdown-embed">





After discover my target flavor, me second step is design all elements around that target.


My favorite part is craft the recipe: to make it enjoyable,it should be simple, easy to replicate, and effective:
I use 13 gram of coffee, with 200 gram water. #ratio is 1:15. The right #ratio to achieve the balance between flavor and mouthfeel for my blend.

My recipe divide into 2 phases, both 2 phases is the immersion method:
 
- First phase: 100 gram of water at 94 celsius degree, spiral pattern of pouring. Total time of the this phase is 1 minute
- This helps coffee powder get saturated, and the extraction happend slowly - smooth happen, keep the flavors slowly transfer from the coffee particle out to my coffee liquid
- At 1 minutes, i will #spin this button at full level to let all the liquid flow down. the drawn time is around 15 seconds. 
- Now I will Spin the button again to close the valve
- And start The second phase: The coffee powder now is wet and partially extracted. I replicate the first phase, with a 100 gram water, but this time at 85 degree celsius, a lower temperature .
- This second immersion phase help me to complete sweetness and structure for final cup, while lower temperature give lower thermal energy, preserving the preferred finish.

The actions of two phases are same, but different water temperature, it translate my sensory target into the cup.

</div></div>



<div class="transclusion internal-embed is-loaded"><div class="markdown-embed">




DESIGN

After discover my target, my second step is design the way to reach that target:

This is the dripper I use today :

This is the Solo body : Its a flat bottom dripper, with 40 degree angle and open base, help the drawn down faster.
This is the Spin base : With the SPIN button: when the valve close, the coffee is immersed. with the valve open, the brew is release through the filter.

When attached it together, I have the Solo Spin : which one rotation spin can changes the brewing environment, so I can control the contact time and drawn down, without adding any complex routine.




</div></div>


<div class="transclusion internal-embed is-loaded"><div class="markdown-embed">




My coffee grind size is 7.5 on Ditting 807LS, which around 700 microns, adapt to my recipe with 2 phases immersion.

</div></div>


<div class="transclusion internal-embed is-loaded"><div class="markdown-embed">




Both coffees are grown at around 2000 meters above sea level. With cool night time temperatures, the coffee cherries undergo a slower maturation. This allow the cherries to produce more sugars, bring a floral, stone fruits complex sweetness

This coffee was roasted 7 days ago, bringing it to its absolute peak flavor profile today. 
I used the Stronghold hybrid roaster for its triple heat system: induction, convection, and radiation. This gentle, internal heat transfer  expands the bean structure from the inside out, bring out clear notes of floral, sweet stone fruit, and a clean, candy-like sweetness.
With 13% development time of 7 roast minutes. My coffee cells open up more, help easier to extract flavors.

</div></div>

For water : I use 35 ppm natural mineral water to highlight vibrant acidity and cup clarity
Today I use water at 2 different temperature : 94 then 80 degree celcius - which I will tell you more details later.
